About

Behind the lens

FrameCraft Studio is the practice of Arjun Vale — photographer, and occasional writer on light and place. After years assisting in London and working commercial jobs in Asia, I returned to focus on work that prioritises connection over spectacle.

I shoot primarily with available light and a small kit. Clients often say the sessions feel unhurried. That’s intentional: the best frames usually arrive when people stop performing.

When I’m not on a job, I’m usually somewhere with a notebook and a slower camera — or editing with a strong coffee and no notifications.
